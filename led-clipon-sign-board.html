LED Clip-On Sign Boards

LED Clip-On Sign Boards are a modern way to transform a regular printed menu or product display into a glowing light sign. PrintzCloud offers LED clip-on boards that highlight your menu, product list or promotional offers clearly for customers.

These boards are commonly used in restaurants, cafes, bakeries, retail shops, food stalls and shopping malls to attract walk-in customers and make menus easier to read.

Features

  • Bright LED illumination for clear visibility
  • Energy efficient lighting
  • Easy clip-on installation
  • Perfect for menus, price lists, and promotional offers
  • Great indoor promotional display
  • Helps grab customer attention quickly
