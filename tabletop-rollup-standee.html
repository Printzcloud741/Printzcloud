Tabletop Roll-Up Standees

Tabletop roll-up standees are smaller than regular standees and are designed to fit perfectly on tables and counters. They are widely used in restaurants, cafes, product display counters, reception desks and event tables where small promotions or menu highlights need to be displayed clearly.

These compact promotional displays help businesses present offers, menu items and product details in a neat and attractive way without taking much space.

Features

  • Compact counter display
  • Lightweight and easy to place
  • Clear high-quality printing
  • Ideal for menu highlights and product offers
  • Reusable display stand
  • Great for indoor advertising

Tabletop roll-up standees are perfect for businesses that want a professional and portable advertising solution for indoor spaces.
